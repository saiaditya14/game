# Backup List — Async Couples Minigame Ideas

Parked ideas from the original async brainstorm. Not the active plan — pull from
here if we ever want turn-based / delayed-reveal games.

1. **Love Letter Dead-Drop** — Leave an encrypted note the other unlocks by answering a question only they'd know.
2. **Two Truths & a Dare** — Two-truths-one-lie, but the lie's penalty is a real-world dare.
3. **Guess-the-Doodle** — Async Pictionary; draw on a canvas, partner guesses later.
4. **Memory Lane** — Upload a photo; partner guesses when and where it was taken.
5. **Mood Weather Report** — Pick today's "weather" for your heart; partner reacts with a care-package.
6. **Reverse Wishlist** — Guess what your partner secretly wants — gifts, dates, cravings.
7. **Kiss-or-Miss (Star Duel)** — Seeded reflex/aim arcade run; compare scores on the same seed.
8. **Story Ping-Pong** — Co-write an absurd story one sentence at a time, no edits.
9. **The Compatibility Vault** — Both secretly answer the same question; points for matching.
10. **Cottage Garden** — A shared plant that only grows when both check in.
11. **Bid for a Date** — Blind-auction weekly love tokens to win date nights.
12. **Hot-or-Cold Hunt** — Hide a treasure behind a real-life location riddle; partner narrows in.
13. **Emoji Charades** — Describe a movie/song/inside-joke using only emoji.
14. **The Confession Jar** — Drop anonymous-until-opened compliments; partner opens one per day.
15. **Rhythm of Us** — Neon rhythm-tap on a shared song; async score battle.
16. **Would-You-Rather Gauntlet** — Would-you-rathers where you also predict the other's answer.
17. **Time Capsule Notes** — Write a message locked until a future date you set.
18. **Duet Sketch Portrait** — Each draws half of the other's face blind; hilarious reveal.
